01 / Context

A real problem.
A focused product.

Plant care often relies on generic schedules, even though each plant and environment may require different follow-up actions.
Approach

Designed around the user journey.

I built a structure of spaces, plants and care history, connected to image-based identification and diagnosis flows. The product is organized for gradual expansion from home plants to larger gardens.

Role & ownership: I worked on the product direction, interface decisions, Flutter implementation, cloud integrations, testing and release workflow. The portfolio describes my own product work, not a third-party client engagement.
