01 / Context

A real problem.
A focused product.

Parents juggle school documents, homework, family schedules and child-specific information across disconnected channels.
Approach

Designed around the user journey.

I developed parent and child experiences with role-based access and dedicated flows for school life. The learning module is designed to explain and guide rather than simply output answers.

Role & ownership: I worked on the product direction, interface decisions, Flutter implementation, cloud integrations, testing and release workflow. The portfolio describes my own product work, not a third-party client engagement.
