01 / Context

A real problem.
A focused product.

Planning meals becomes repetitive when ingredients, expiration dates, recipes and shopping are handled separately.
Approach

Designed around the user journey.

I connected pantry and recipe workflows inside a Flutter app, with cloud data, receipt/photo inputs and controlled AI-assisted recommendations. Reuse and caching help keep media handling efficient.

Role & ownership: I worked on the product direction, interface decisions, Flutter implementation, cloud integrations, testing and release workflow. The portfolio describes my own product work, not a third-party client engagement.
