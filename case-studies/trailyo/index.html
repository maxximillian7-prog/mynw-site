01 / Context

A real problem.
A focused product.

Discovering what to do in Portugal often means jumping between event calendars, maps, local websites and community channels. Trailyo brings these fragmented discovery paths into a single mobile product.
Approach

Designed around the user journey.

I designed a map-first experience with browseable categories, location-aware exploration, community interactions and a multilingual content layer. The work combines Flutter product UX with external data sources, moderation and app-store delivery.

Role & ownership: I worked on the product direction, interface decisions, Flutter implementation, cloud integrations, testing and release workflow. The portfolio describes my own product work, not a third-party client engagement.
