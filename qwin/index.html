About Qwin

Qwin is a MyNow Apps marketplace for listing and discovering items in Portugal. Sellers choose the listing details, images, contact preferences and external platforms. Qwin does not process buyer payments.

OLX and Etsy are independent platforms governed by their own rules, eligibility, fees and moderation. Connecting a platform never guarantees publication. Etsy accepts only items compliant with its Creativity Standards.

Privacy policy

Qwin is provided by MyNow Apps, an independent developer based in Portugal. This notice describes how the app handles personal data. Last reviewed: 11 November 2026.

Data processed

  • Account credentials are handled by the authentication provider. Qwin stores your user ID, profile, listing details, photographs and the public contact information you choose.
  • Messages, offers, saved listings, reporting and moderation records are stored to provide marketplace functions.
  • AI-assisted descriptions and images are sent to the configured AI service only after you request or approve the processing. Do not upload others' personal data without permission.
  • When you connect OLX or Etsy, platform authorization tokens are processed by the protected server. Publication and conversation data may be exchanged only as required for requested functions.

Purpose, protection and retention

Processing supports account management, your requested marketplace services, fraud prevention, support and legal obligations. Necessary processing relies on contract performance and legitimate safety interests; optional marketing requires applicable consent. Data are retained only as needed for these purposes and applicable law. Deletion may be limited by active transactions, legal claims or fraud investigations.

Your rights

Under applicable GDPR rules you may request access, correction, export, deletion, restriction or objection by contacting support. You may complain to Portugal's supervisory authority, CNPD. Platform-held data must also be managed with the respective platform.

Terms of use

By using Qwin you agree to provide accurate information, have the rights to sell and photograph your items, respect other members, and comply with Portuguese law and each connected marketplace's terms. The seller and buyer negotiate and complete transactions directly; Qwin is not a party to the sale, does not receive or hold payment, and does not guarantee item condition or delivery.

Do not post counterfeit, stolen, illegal or unsafe products, misleading photos, spam, harassment or listings infringing third-party rights. Moderators may remove reported content or suspend accounts to maintain safety. External publication, prices, listing limits, charges and eligibility remain controlled by the external platforms.

Your listing photographs and descriptions remain yours or their respective owners; you grant Qwin the non-exclusive permission required to display your listings and relay approved publications. You can revoke a connected platform through account settings, subject to its separate removal policies.

Marketplace safety and moderation

  • Inspect items before payment and verify the seller, especially for high-value products.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes, bank login details or identity documents in marketplace chat.
  • Use safe public meeting places; do not pay through unexpected third-party links.
  • Use the in-app report function or contact support for prohibited items, harassment, infringement or suspected scams. Provide the listing ID and supporting information.
  • Users may contest moderation actions through support. Safety and law-enforcement requests are evaluated under applicable law.

AI-generated text and images

AI may suggest listing text, category matches and alternative product photos. You decide whether to accept or publish each result. AI can be inaccurate and should never invent defects, brands, conditions or supplied accessories. Keep truthful originals and check all details before publishing.

AI-adjusted listing photographs should be identified in expanded-image view. Sellers must not conceal damage or materially misrepresent products. The original and proposed alternatives can be reviewed before approval. Requests may be subject to quotas, paid features and content safeguards.

Etsy's separate Creativity Standards and policies determine whether AI-assisted or seller-designed work is eligible there. Qwin listing eligibility is not automatic permission to publish on Etsy.

Delete listings and accounts

For an individual listing, open My listings, select your listing and choose Edit / Delete from connected platforms. Qwin attempts supported external deletion first; it will not report success unless the platform confirms. Active sales or legal holds may require resolution.

To request permanent account deletion, contact support from the email associated with your account with subject “Qwin account deletion”. We will verify ownership, explain any required transaction or legal retention restrictions, and assist with removal. Separate OLX/Etsy accounts and their data must be deleted at the respective services.

Deleting the Qwin app from a device does not delete the online account. Disconnecting an external platform does not delete listings already hosted there.

Website cookies and storage

This documentation page uses the browser's local storage to remember your language choice. Other cookies or site analytics, if enabled elsewhere on MyNow Apps, follow the hosting site's applicable policy. Qwin app credentials are not collected by this documentation page.
