About Qwin

Qwin is a marketplace application in development by MyNow Apps in Portugal. Internal listings, search, filters, chats, offers and favourites belong to one Qwin experience. OLX seller connections are optional and subject to platform permissions. Qwin does not process payments.

Qwin is not publicly launched. Qwin Market is available in testing; OLX is the optional external integration. No external platform has guaranteed publication, messaging, affiliate payouts or checkout through Qwin.

Privacy policy

Qwin is operated in Portugal by Maksym Smyrnov, trading as MyNow Apps. Privacy enquiries: support@mynw.app. This notice describes how Qwin processes personal data. Last reviewed: 14 November 2026.

Data processed

  • Account credentials are handled by the authentication provider. Qwin stores your user ID, profile, listing details, photographs and the public contact information you choose.
  • Messages, offers, saved listings, reporting and moderation records are stored to provide marketplace functions.
  • AI-assisted descriptions and images are sent to the configured AI service only after you request or approve the processing. Do not upload others' personal data without permission.
  • If you connect OLX Portugal, Qwin exchanges information required for authorized publishing, account data and messaging. Access tokens are encrypted on the server, not embedded in the app. Disconnecting Qwin does not remove existing OLX listings or your OLX account.
  • Search and filters use listing details such as category, city and optional delivery settings. The app may process diagnostic, security and support records. Your private home address is not published by default.

Purpose, protection and retention

We process account, listing, message and support data to provide requested services (contract), prevent abuse and protect users (legitimate interests) and comply with legal duties. Optional marketing, if introduced, requires separate permission where applicable. Data are retained while necessary for service functions; after deletion, records needed for disputes, security or legal obligations may be kept for the applicable period and then removed or anonymised. Ask support about retention of particular records.

Visibility, recipients and chat photos

Your public listings may display a name, general area, price and photographs. Your Qwin phone number is optional and is shown to other users only if you enable a public contact option; OLX may require a phone number for OLX listings. Qwin messages are intended for their participants. Photographs attached to chat are hosted at hard-to-guess links, so avoid sensitive documents or personal information. Hosting, authentication, storage, AI processing on request and authorized OLX operations involve third-party service providers. Contact support for details about international transfers and safeguards.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, correction, portability where applicable, erasure, restriction, objection and withdrawal of optional consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact support@mynw.app; reasonable identity verification may apply. You may complain to Portugal’s supervisory authority, CNPD (www.cnpd.pt). Records held by OLX must be managed with OLX.

Terms of use

By using Qwin you agree to provide accurate information, have the rights to sell and photograph your items, respect other members, and comply with Portuguese law and each connected marketplace's terms. The seller and buyer negotiate and complete transactions directly; Qwin is not a party to the sale, does not receive or hold payment, and does not guarantee item condition or delivery.

Do not post counterfeit, stolen, illegal or unsafe products, misleading photos, spam, harassment or listings infringing third-party rights. Moderators may remove reported content or suspend accounts to maintain safety. External publication, prices, listing limits, charges and eligibility remain controlled by the external platforms.

Your listing photographs and descriptions remain yours or their respective owners. You grant Qwin the limited permission necessary to display approved listings and, if available and requested, relay them to a connected marketplace. Disconnecting does not automatically remove an existing external listing.

Offers and messages are negotiation requests, not paid orders. Qwin does not process payments. OLX publishing requires user authorization and may undergo moderation before an advert becomes visible. OLX sets any listing fees and renewal conditions; Qwin leaves auto-renewal disabled unless you explicitly enable it. Delivery, returns, warranties and disputes are governed by applicable law and the respective parties or platform.

Marketplace safety and moderation

  • Inspect items before payment and verify the seller, especially for high-value products.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes, bank login credentials, payment card numbers or identity documents in a marketplace chat, including photo attachments.
  • Use safe public meeting places; do not pay through unexpected third-party links.
  • Use the in-app report function or contact support for prohibited items, harassment, infringement or suspected scams. Provide the listing ID and supporting information.
  • To report potentially illegal or unsafe content, send support the listing URL or ID, an explanation and available evidence. You may contest Qwin moderation decisions through support. Notices and requests are reviewed under applicable law.

AI-generated text and images

AI may suggest listing text, category matches and alternative product photos. You decide whether to accept or publish each result. AI can be inaccurate and should never invent defects, brands, conditions or supplied accessories. Keep truthful originals and check all details before publishing.

AI-adjusted listing photographs should be identified in expanded-image view. Sellers must not conceal damage or materially misrepresent products. The original and proposed alternatives can be reviewed before approval. Requests may be subject to quotas, paid features and content safeguards.

AI assistance never removes the duty to verify product accuracy or the rules of a connected marketplace.

Delete listings and accounts

For an individual listing, open My listings, select your listing and choose Edit / Delete from connected platforms. Qwin attempts supported external deletion first; it will not report success unless the platform confirms. Active sales or legal holds may require resolution.

Use Profile → Delete Account after removing active listings, or email support@mynw.app from your registered address with subject “Qwin account deletion” if the in-app process is unavailable. Identity verification and necessary legal retention may apply. Deleting Qwin does not delete your OLX account.

Uninstalling Qwin does not delete the online account. Disconnecting OLX or a previously connected account does not automatically remove external listings. Verify removal with the relevant service.

External marketplaces & affiliate disclosure

Qwin is independent from OLX. OLX controls its own terms, user authorization, listing eligibility, fees, moderation and checkout. This website shows only illustrative listings, not an active product feed.

OLX Portugal

OLX Portugal is an optional integration for authorized sellers. Qwin can prepare and submit listings, read permitted conversations and request status updates. OLX controls moderation, fees, listing expiry and service availability; an advert may initially be under review.

Qwin is independent from OLX Portugal and is not endorsed by it. Marketplace functions depend on account authorization, API permissions and provider availability. Qwin cannot guarantee publication or immediate message delivery, and never marks an external purchase as paid without confirmation.

Website cookies and storage

Qwin website uses local storage to remember the selected language. It may load licensed fallback photography from Unsplash and fonts from Google Fonts, which can receive technical connection data such as your IP address and browser details. This information site does not request marketplace passwords. No optional advertising cookies are needed to use this site.
