Questions

What working together looks like.

A few practical points before your first message.

Do you work alone?

Yes. You communicate directly with the developer responsible for your project. Larger requests may need to be divided into phases or scoped differently.

Can you build for both iOS and Android?

Yes. I generally use Flutter for a shared mobile codebase, with platform-specific integrations where required. Store review and approval remain subject to Apple and Google policies.

Can you integrate AI into an existing workflow?

Yes, where a useful, well-defined workflow can be built: image or document interpretation, structured outputs, recommendation engines and assistant-style interactions.

How much will my app cost?

Custom development projects start from €5,000. A reliable fixed-price estimate follows a short discussion of the problem, scope, integrations and milestones.
