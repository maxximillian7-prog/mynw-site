FAQ

Simple answers before you install.

What can I share with NowWhat?

Compatible documents, screenshots, images, PDFs and text can be opened or shared into NowWhat. Voice notes can also be recorded inside the app.

Does NowWhat replace professional advice?

No. AI output can be incomplete or wrong. NowWhat is a productivity assistant, not a substitute for medical, legal, financial, tax or other professional advice.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. A dedicated account deletion path is available from the app and from the account deletion page.
