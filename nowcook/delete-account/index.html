Delete your NowCook account and associated data

Last updated: September 26, 2026

You can start an account-deletion request from the NowCook app or use this page if you no longer have access to the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@mynw.app from the email associated with your NowCook account when possible.
  2. Use the subject Delete my NowCook account.
  3. Include the email address used for the NowCook account.
  4. We may ask for reasonable verification that you own the account before deletion is completed.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

After a valid request is completed, the NowCook account and associated server-side user data are deleted, except for information that must be retained for legitimate legal, security or abuse-prevention reasons.

Local device data

Some settings or cached content may remain locally on a device until the app data is cleared or the app is removed.

Signing out is not deletion

Signing out or resetting app settings does not delete the NowCook account or cloud data.
