This Privacy Policy explains how the current version of NowCook accesses, collects, uses, stores and shares user data.

1. Data we may process

  • Account identifier and email address when an account is used.
  • Food profile information such as serving preferences, allergies, dietary preferences and restrictions.
  • Pantry items, quantities, expiry dates and categories.
  • Recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, cooking history, ratings and saved preferences.
  • Text submitted to AI features and the minimum relevant context needed to answer the request.
  • Transcribed speech when the user chooses voice input.
  • Images the user chooses to provide to an app feature.
  • Local app settings, notification preferences and timer settings.

2. Why we use data

  • To provide and synchronize NowCook features.
  • To generate recipes, meal plans, answers and cooking guidance.
  • To calculate missing ingredients and update pantry quantities after user confirmation.
  • To provide timers and expiry reminders.
  • To preserve user preferences and cooking history.

3. Service providers and AI processing

NowCook uses cloud infrastructure and server-side AI providers to deliver core features. When an AI feature is used, the submitted request and relevant context may be sent to these service providers for processing. Providers and models may change as the service evolves.

4. Device permissions

Microphone access is used only when the user starts voice input. Photo or camera access is used only when the user chooses an image-related feature. Notification permission is used for reminders and timers where supported.

5. Security

Network communication between the app and backend services uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to account data is protected by authentication and server-side access controls.

6. Retention and deletion

Account data is retained while needed to provide the service or until deletion is requested. A user may request deletion of their NowCook account and associated server-side data at mynw.app/nowcook/delete-account/. Some information may be retained only where required for legal, security or abuse-prevention reasons.

7. User choices

Voice input, image features and notifications are optional. Signing out or resetting local settings does not delete the account or cloud data.

8. Children

The Kids Kitchen interface is intended for use together with a responsible adult. Adults should supervise device permissions, requests and cooking activity.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: support@mynw.app.
